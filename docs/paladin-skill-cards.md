# Paladin skill cards

48 supplied icons from assets/free-paladin-pixel-art-skill-icons/PNG are catalog IDs 514–561, roster version 14. All are neutral, single-use, one-copy cards without stars. All 48 cards have distinct names and configurations tied to the icon art. Twelve trigger families cover single-target/group healing, cleansing, starting armor, lethal prevention, melee retaliation, any-attack retaliation, stun, fire, ranged reflection, multi-hit suppression and weakening. Values/costs and bilingual descriptions are recorded in data/paladin_skills.json. Healing and cleansing wait until they have a valid target. Starting-armor skills consume at battle start; other skills consume only when triggered. Shop rows allow at most one optional support offer alongside the mandatory trial Healing Totem, preserving space for hero offers.

Shop, collection and deck previews show the supplied icon on the assigned Deco/FagelTomten frame. On the field SkillCardVisual renders the entire card upright and readable for both teams, floating by ±1.5 world units. Cards use original assets/effects/cards/rip/cardRIP_* masks, ordered 01→015 at 18 FPS, to remove the frame and icon together when their HP reaches zero. The card stays visible while ripping and hides at completion. Replay seeking can hide directly without replaying the effect.

Tests: paladin_skill_cards.tscn validates all 48 icons, full-card battlefield rendering, both teams, float and rip-to-hidden lifecycle. Backend special card tests exercise purchasing, deployment, trigger logic and no merging for every support kind.
